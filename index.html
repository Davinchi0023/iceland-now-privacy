Iceland NOW — Privacy policy

Updated: 15 October 2026 · Contact: davin4i0023@gmail.com

Iceland NOW has no account registration, advertising, analytics or developer-operated backend. Shopping lists, manually entered prices, budgets, saved places, personal plans, settings and cached results are stored locally on your device. This release disables Android app backup.

Location is optional and used only while you request it in Nearby. You can select a city without granting permission. No background location is requested. When searching — after you tap Find places or automatically after you enter text in Nearby — the search center coordinates (your location or chosen city), radius and selected categories are sent over HTTPS to overpass-api.de, an OpenStreetMap data service. The coordinates also appear in local search cache keys. Third-party services receive your IP address and may retain request logs under their own policies. We cannot guarantee ephemeral processing by these services.

Weather requests over HTTPS to xmlweather.vedur.is use the fixed Reykjavík station, not your device location. Search text used to filter results remains on your device. We do not automatically upload shopping lists or personal plans.

Map, directions, website, phone, sharing and calendar actions open another app or service only when you select them. Those services receive the place, destination, shared text or event details needed for your chosen action and apply their own privacy policies. Calendar entries are added through the system calendar editor; the app does not read your calendar or contacts. Support email is sent using your email app. Information you send for support is used to answer your request and retained only as needed for that purpose; request deletion by email.

Local data remains until you remove it or clear the app's storage in Android settings. Revoking location permission does not remove previously cached results. Clearing app storage removes all locally stored app data. You can ask about privacy or deletion of support correspondence at the contact above. There are no server accounts to delete.

Network data services: https://overpass-api.de/ and https://www.vedur.is/ External links, maps and Android system services are governed by their providers. This independent app is not an official government app or affiliated with the listed businesses. We will update this policy when data practices change.

Official calendars are fetched over HTTPS from visitreykjavik.is and www.harpa.is when opening events or global search if the cache needs updating, and after manual refresh. Requests do not include your search text, plans or location; the websites receive your IP address and technical request data. Titles, dates, venues and links are cached on your device.

Place correction drafts are stored locally (up to 100). The app can open a prefilled support email to davin4i0023@gmail.com; you review and send it in your email app. Opening the email does not confirm delivery. Reports are reviewed manually and do not automatically update the catalogue or OpenStreetMap. You may also share a draft through another app. Drafts can be deleted in Nearby → My correction drafts. Do not include sensitive personal information. Reports you send are handled as support correspondence.
